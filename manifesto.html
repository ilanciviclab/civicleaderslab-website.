The manifesto

A new standard for civic leadership.

The world has changed. The way we lead our cities must change with it.

The moment

People are tired of politics without leadership.

They want vision. They want trust. They want character. They want to be heard, and to feel safe in the hands of those who lead them.

That is where a new kind of leadership can start. Most of today’s civic leadership systems were built for another era. They are run by political habit rather than leadership, and they are not keeping pace with the fast-moving human challenges of our age.

What is at stake

America’s identity is at stake.

America is at risk of losing its American identity.

Across the country, well-funded and highly organized movements are gaining ground in city halls, often out of sight of the residents they affect. Some of that funding comes from outside the United States.

Some of these movements are driven by ideologies that openly reject America’s founding values, and say plainly that their aim is not to join American life but to replace it. We take them at their word.

Our answer is to protect what makes America, America: its founding principles, its freedoms, and the right of every person to live by their faith under one law for all.

What they push

  • Confusion over the most basic truths about gender.
  • Weakening the family as the foundation of American life.
  • An economy of dependency, asking for money instead of earning it.
  • Open-border policies that reward illegal immigration.

If we do not act boldly now, we risk losing what makes America, America. Our answer is not anger. It is a new generation of leaders.

Our values

Nonpartisan. Not without values.

We do not serve a party. Our vision is rooted in the American and Western values that made this country extraordinary, and every leader we develop will carry them.

True leadership

Leaders who serve before they rule, act with character, and deliver real results.

Lead with honor

We build our message on our strength, not on our opponents’ weakness. No insults, no humiliation, no celebrating others’ failures. We are honest about our competitors’ strengths and weaknesses, and we prove why our vision is better.

A purpose for every person

We build the next generation of humanity by leading people to their purpose path.

Love of country

Pride in America’s founding principles, and the duty to pass them on stronger than we found them.

Love of people and community

Every resident matters. A city is only as strong as the bonds between its people.

Man and woman

We recognize two sexes, male and female, and honor the dignity of every person.

Freedom of faith

Every person is free to believe, worship, and live by their faith without interference.

Prosperity through free enterprise

Wealth is built by people free to create, compete, and keep the fruits of their work.

Equal rights for all

One standard of rights and opportunity for every citizen, with no exceptions and no favorites.

Justice under law

Laws applied fairly, safe streets, and accountability for everyone, including those in power.

Innovation and adaptation

Embracing new technologies, new ways of thinking, and new ways of living.

We will raise leaders who keep America, America, and build its future.

Our vision

New standards for civic leadership.

Leaders, not politicians.

We believe cities should be led by people with natural leadership ability, ambition, creativity, courage, and a genuine desire to serve.

An entrepreneurial spirit.

Cities should run with efficiency, speed, creativity, critical thinking, and strong growth, always grounded in what residents truly need and want.

At the speed of life.

A city leader’s decision can change residents’ lives within a week. The world is moving forward faster than ever. Leadership and governance must move with it.

The standard of public leadership can be raised: deliberately, visibly, and for good.

Authenticity over political correctness

Mean what you say. Say what you mean.

Political correctness began as a tool of politics: a way to communicate, negotiate, and keep tensions low. Over time it became a norm, and then an expectation. Rules about what you can and cannot say, whether you mean it or not, just to avoid offending the other side or inflaming the moment.

That is the opposite of authenticity. It is the opposite of integrity. Leaders who weigh every word to avoid offense slowly stop telling people the truth, and people can feel it.

Diplomacy has its place at the negotiating table. It cannot be our vision, our public policy, or the standard for our society.

Honesty and respect are not opposites.
We will lead with both, never with scripts.

Why cities

Cities are the laboratories of the future.

At the city level, leadership becomes visible. Residents see it in the park, the street, the permit process, the neighborhood, the public space, and the quality of everyday government. They can trace the line from a decision, to an action, to a result.

That is why residents should never simply receive government decisions. They deserve to understand them, feel their impact, influence them, and help shape what comes next.

DECISION→ACTION→RESULT

Cities can become places where ideas are tested quickly, results are measured clearly, mistakes are corrected honestly, and successful models are shared openly.

Why now

People have not stopped caring. They have stopped believing.

72,000

votes cast in the June 2024 Las Vegas mayoral primary, in a city of more than 640,000 residents.

People don’t believe in politics, so they don’t show up. Across America’s largest cities, the typical mayoral voter is close to 60 years old, and voters over 65 are about ten times more likely to vote than those aged 18 to 34.

That silence is not apathy. It is an opening. It means a new vision can reach the people politics has never reached, especially voters between 18 and 40, who vote least and have the most future at stake.

We will engage a new generation of voters with new ideas, fitted to who they are, and to where the world is going.

The AI revolution

Leadership for the age of AI.

The world is moving into the AI revolution. In every area of city life, it opens a new opportunity: to know residents better, understand their struggles, and shape solutions around each of them.

Know every resident

Understand the needs, wants, struggles, and problems of the people we serve.

Personal service

Treat every resident like a valued customer, with help tailored to them.

See problems early

Identify problems before they happen, and act before they grow.

Faster, better solutions

Solve problems in days, not months, with answers that fit each case.

A more efficient city

Less waste, less bureaucracy, more results for every dollar.

Growth for every adult

Personal programs for adults who truly want to change their lives.

AI can be a powerful force for good. We will master it, and keep it working for people, always.

Our commitments

The New Standard.

The commitments that define the leadership we will build, test, and share.

Leadership before politics.

Politics is part of governing. It must never become its purpose. Vision, people, responsibility, and results come first.

Residents first.

Every system is measured by the quality of life it creates for the people it serves.

An entrepreneurial spirit.

Government does not need to become a business. But it can ask what great entrepreneurs ask: What problem are we solving? Who are we serving? Can this be simpler? How do we measure success?

Public value is the return.

In business, the return may be profit. In public service, it is a better quality of life, greater opportunity, stronger communities, and greater trust.

The right people in the right roles.

Better systems begin with people who know where they are strongest, placed where their strengths create the greatest value.

Test, measure, improve.

Ideas become pilots. Pilots become evidence, measured with data and AI. Evidence becomes the model.

Share openly.

If a solution works, it should not belong to one city. What works is shared. What fails is documented. What can be improved invites collaboration.

The future of our cities will be shaped by the people willing to lead them.

Let’s build that future together.
