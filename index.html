Who we are

A leadership institute.

Civic Leaders Lab is where the future of civic leadership is built. We bring together the best minds, resources, and visions, and turn them into a living plan for how cities should be led, and the leaders ready to lead them.

  • We gather the best minds and the boldest ideas on civic leadership.
  • We define the future of city leadership, and turn it into a real plan.
  • We discover natural leaders, and we develop them.
  • We give them the knowledge, skills, and network to lead.
  • And we share what works, so every city can rise.

The moment

People are tired of politics without leadership.

They want vision. They want trust. They want character. They want to be heard, and to feel safe in the hands of those who lead them.

42%

trust the people who hold or run for public office.

67%

trust their local government’s ability to handle local problems.

People have lost faith in politicians, not in the power of their city.

Source: Gallup Governance poll, September 2026
