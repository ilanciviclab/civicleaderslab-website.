Months 1–3

The Inner Core

Every leader begins with themselves.

In the first three months, each fellow does the deep work of self-awareness: discovering their natural gifts, facing their strengths and weaknesses honestly, and defining their true purpose. Their why must be strong, and it will be challenged again and again through personal coaching, retreats, and intensive seminars. This is also where we learn whether a fellow is real, and leading for the right reasons.

Personal coachingPurpose and values workOpening retreatBreakthrough seminars

Months 4–6

The Voice

A leader who cannot be heard cannot lead.

Fellows learn to speak with power and authenticity: on a stage, in front of a camera, in a council chamber, or across a kitchen table. They master public speaking, presence, storytelling, media, and social media, and they practice in front of real audiences until their message is clear, confident, and unmistakably their own.

Public speakingBody language and presenceStorytellingMedia and public relationsSocial media mastery

Months 7–9

The Strategist

Vision needs a map.

Fellows learn how cities really work: how budgets are built, how decisions are made, how institutions move, and which civic models succeed or fail, and why. They study political science and public policy, social and development strategy, fundraising, negotiation, and coalition building, so they can turn a vision into a plan that earns support and delivers results.

Civic systems and policyPolitical scienceSocial strategyDevelopment strategyFundraisingNegotiation and coalitions

Months 10–12

The Builder

Ideas are only real when they are built.

In the final quarter, each fellow takes on a real problem facing a real city and designs the solution, with a team, a budget, a timeline, and measurable results. They learn to attract top talent, build systems that scale, leverage their connections, and lead with AI and data. The year ends at Civic Demo Day, where fellows present their plans to partners, experts, and the public.

Attracting top talentSystems that scaleLeveraging connectionsLeading with AI and dataCivic Demo Day

After twelve months, every graduate is ready to carry the message into any city: to put these qualities into action, create change, and spread the movement.

After the program

The leader we build.

Character first. Purpose always. Vision ahead. Results that prove it.

Character

Who the leader is
Integrity
Says what they mean, does what they say, in private as in public.
Courage
Makes the hard call and stands behind it.
Humility
Strong enough to say “I was wrong,” and to learn from anyone.
Honor
Builds on their own strength, never on an opponent’s weakness.

Purpose

Why they lead
Service
Leadership is a duty to people, not a reward.
Purpose-driven
Leads from their true self and core values, not from polls or pressure.
Love of country and community
Leads out of care for America and for the people of their city.

Vision

Where they lead
Vision
Sees the future first, and gives people a clear picture of where they are going.
Curiosity
Always asks why, explores the new, and learns from every source.
Inspiration
Moves people to believe again, and calls out the best in every resident.

Execution

How they lead
Ownership
Full responsibility for results. No excuses, no blaming.
Entrepreneurial drive
Fast, creative, efficient: ideas turned into results.
Growth
Never stops learning, adapting, and becoming a better version of themselves.

Beyond these virtues, every graduate will be ready: ready to lead, to innovate, to change the status quo, to disrupt, and to build things as they should be. Confident, connected, and fully equipped, they will have everything they need to start.
