Our work

What we will build.

Five programs that turn a new standard of leadership into tested, shareable practice.

The Future City Leadership Model

A practical operating system for modern civic leadership: vision, decision-making, accountability, culture, resident experience, performance, and technology.

The Future City Leadership Fellowship

Do not wait for great leaders to find public service. Go find them. We will recruit high-potential people from technology, business, sports, education, entrepreneurship, nonprofits, and community life, and teach them how cities work, how decisions are made, how to build teams, and how to turn vision into implementation. See the Fellowship.

The City Innovation Lab

The best minds from entrepreneurship, technology, design, academia, psychology, and public administration, designing and testing practical solutions, from permitting and resident services to AI, transparency, and small-business support.

Civic Performance and Human Potential

Helping civic institutions move beyond filling positions, and place people where their strengths, skills, and motivation create the greatest value.

The Publishing Engine

Tested knowledge, not opinions: playbooks, case studies, scorecards, tools, and an honest record of what worked and what did not.

How we work

Research the present. Create the future. Share what works.

RESEARCH→CREATE→TEST→PUBLISH→IMPLEMENT↻ and repeat

We turn ideas into pilots, measure results with data and AI, and learn directly from residents and city teams. Then we publish what we learn, openly.
