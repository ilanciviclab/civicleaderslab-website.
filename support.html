The invitation

Not a donation. An investment in a legacy.

Civic Leaders Lab is a nonprofit. But supporting it is not just another way to give back. It is an opportunity to help set a new standard of leadership, and a new future for our cities.

The first year

$1.8M

to launch the first year in Las Vegas.

The first class

Prepare our first class of leaders in Las Vegas, ready to serve as mayor and as city council members.

The hub

Establish a center of leadership: offices, meeting rooms, a media room, a social media center, and a podcast studio. A place where people come to bring ideas and think big together.

The team

Build a core team of seven exceptional minds to run the operation.

The model

Within one year, deliver a complete model of how the future city should look and be led.

Founding partners

Join a small circle of founding partners.

We are inviting a small circle of founding partners to help launch the first Fellowship, the first city pilots in Las Vegas, and the first open playbooks for a new era of civic leadership.

A detailed budget and program plan is available to founding partners.

The future of our cities will be shaped by the quality of the people willing to lead them. Let’s build that future together.
